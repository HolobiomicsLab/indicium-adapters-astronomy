## [0.1.0] — 2026-07-30

### Added
- `AstronomyAdapter` (`api_version=1`), registered on the `indicium.adapters` entry point.
  Declares `domain_id` / `primary_domain` = `astronomy`, 20 snake_case domain qualifiers, and
  the prefix map needed to expand astronomy CURIEs.
- Unified Astronomy Thesaurus concept enrichment. `enrich_claim` attaches a `UAT:` CURIE to any
  claim slot naming a UAT concept, with a `semapv:LexicalMatching` justification. Pure: it
  returns a new dict and never overwrites a term another adapter has set.
- Vendored UAT v6.0.0 index (2411 concepts, 4219 preferred + alternative labels) with a
  provenance block, regenerated by `scripts/generate_uat_index.py`. The whole thesaurus rather
  than a curated subset, so an unmatched term means "not in the UAT" instead of "not in our
  list".
- `subdomains` (11) and `techniques` (27) derived from the thesaurus itself — its level-1
  branches and the leaves of "Astronomical techniques" — so they move with a UAT release.
- `ontology_terms_unmatched`: slots that carried text but resolved to nothing, so a miss stays
  distinguishable from "not attempted".
- `ontology_term_branches`: the UAT level-1 branch of each matched concept. The thesaurus is
  not disjoint from other sciences' words — it contains *Mass spectrometry* (`UAT:2094`),
  *Regression*, *Neural networks* — so the branch lets a consumer discount a term that is
  shared method vocabulary. `_uat.GENERIC_BRANCHES` names the branches concerned.

### Deliberately not included
- **Accession recognition.** Under ADR 0001 the canonical accession table in `asb-domain-core`
  owns those patterns; the four astronomy families live there. A test greps this package's own
  source and fails if an accession shape appears.
- **Operations and data-format vocabularies.** Astronomy has no EDAM counterpart (confirmed
  with a domain expert, 2026-07-30). Formats stay in the ASB domain profile's `format_registry`.

### Known limitations
- Matching is exact after case-folding and whitespace collapse, so a paraphrase does not
  resolve. Substring or fuzzy matching over 2411 terms produces confident wrong CURIEs, and a
  wrong identifier is worse than none.
- `requires-python >= 3.11` here, but `indicium-adapters` itself requires `>= 3.12`. Until that
  floor comes down the adapter cannot be installed into the AgenticScienceBuilder environment
  (Python 3.11), which is where its classification vocabulary is consumed. Verified working
  under Python 3.12: `discover_adapters()` returns it and `isinstance(a, DomainAdapter)` holds.
